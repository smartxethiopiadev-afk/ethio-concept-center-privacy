Welcome to Ethio Concept Center. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and process your information when you use our mobile application.

By downloading, installing, or using the Ethio Concept Center application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with a smooth, efficient, and personalized experience, we collect specific information, including:

  • Phone Number: We collect your phone number during user account registration, authentication, or verification. This allows us to uniquely identify your user profile, manage account access, and ensure security.
  • Session & Authentication Tokens: We collect and generate unique session tokens to keep you securely logged in across app launches without requiring re-authentication each time.

2. App Functionality & System Permissions

Our application requires access to specific device capabilities to deliver its core features:

  • Internet Access: The app utilizes your network connection (Wi-Fi or Mobile Data) to fetch content updates, synchronize user state, perform user authentication, and transfer online dynamic resources.
  • Local File System Storage & Local Cache: Ethio Concept Center uses your device's internal storage system to save offline configuration files, user session data, cached lessons/documents, and application state files.

3. Offline Mode & Data Storage

Offline Functionality: Ethio Concept Center is engineered to work seamlessly both online and offline.

When operating without an active internet connection, the application stores and reads necessary data from your device's local file system. Locally stored files are managed securely by the app and remain stored on your device. Once an internet connection is re-established, the app synchronizes local session updates with our secure backend servers.

4. How We Use Your Information

We strictly utilize the collected data for operational and functional purposes:

  • To verify your identity using your phone number during login.
  • To maintain active user sessions and manage profile settings.
  • To provide offline availability of app resources via local file caching.
  • To sync app updates, progress, and preferences when online.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical or security issues.

5. Data Security

We value your trust in providing us with your personal details. We apply industry-standard security measures to safeguard both online network communications and locally stored session/file data. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Third-Party Services

The app may use reliable third-party infrastructure and service providers (such as cloud hosting and database backends) to process login requests, maintain sessions, and deliver server resources. These third parties access your information solely to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Your session tokens and offline storage files remain stored locally on your device until you clear the app data or uninstall the application. You can request account or phone number record removal by contacting our support team.

8. Children's Privacy

Ethio Concept Center does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take necessary actions.
